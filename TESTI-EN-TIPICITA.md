## 1. .Thread (Trama) — le tre schede

1. **From local to global** — Festivals, showcases and thematic forums —
   food, tourism, design, research, sustainability. Not separate
   chapters: the same question, seen from different angles.
2. **A glocal co-creation lab** — Shared projects with universities,
   companies and institutions. Not to tell Italy to the world, but to
   build it together with those who see it from the outside.
3. **A platform for the territory** — A network connecting people,
   places and stories — the same three hundred public and private
   partners that stand behind every edition.

## 2. .Essence (Essenza) — le 10 card

01 (lead) — **Typicity is not a label.** It is the trace a territory
leaves in what it creates.
[ADAT: "typicity" è il termine tecnico del mondo del vino — registro
giusto per un pubblico food & business.]

02 (key, manifesto) — Tipicità is *a cultural response* to an
environmental stimulus.

03 (the trace) — Ciauscolo — the soft, spreadable salami of these
hills — is not typical of the Marche because someone decided it. It is
because someone, before anyone else, interpreted in that form **the
climate, the farming, the tempo of one precise hillside** — that one,
and no other.
[ADAT: micro-glossa "the soft, spreadable salami of these hills" —
l'unico inserto esplicativo concesso, il lettore estero non conosce il
ciauscolo.]

04 (the trace) — And it holds for a salami as for software: a dev house
in Mumbai and one in Tolentino will never work the same way. Not a
difference in skill. **A difference in territory.**

05 — photo card (invariata).

06 (the crossing) — Yet no typicity survives alone. The tomato arrived
from another continent before becoming **more Neapolitan than Naples
itself**. And Italian cuisine is a UNESCO candidate for the opposite
reason to the one you would expect: because it is a global product —
changed, and still changing.

07 (key) — The local does not survive by sealing itself off from the
world — *it survives by crossing it.*

08 (the tension) — **Trajectories** and **native**: two words pulling in
opposite directions — which is why they hold each other upright.
Trajectory is airborne: routes, markets, a world in motion. Native is
warm, almost primal: the instinct of a place, before it becomes a story.

09 (the tension) — Tipicità stands exactly in between: **it tells the
global through the local.**

10 (key, finale) — Today Tipicità is becoming something else again:
*the festival of native trajectories*.

Kickers: la traccia → `the trace` · l'attraversamento → `the crossing` ·
la tensione → `the tension`.

## 3. .Engine (Motore)

Label del banco: `THE FORMULA`. Nodi: **Trajectories · Fuel · Impact ·
Gears**.

- Trajectories — Made in Marche travels beyond regional borders: from
  the stages of Fermo to the fairs of **Dubai, New York, Tokyo** — not
  isolated showcases, stops on the same route.
- Fuel — **~300** `partners in network` — Institutions, companies,
  universities: a **public-private partnership** that co-designs every
  edition.
- Impact — Every edition converts into concrete opportunities:
  **visibility for brands**, access to national and international
  markets, **relationships that stay active** beyond the festival's
  three days.
- Gears — Nine themes run through every edition: · Territorial
  development · Biodiversity & sustainability · Experiential tourism ·
  Food heritage & innovation · Creative manufacturing & Made in Italy ·
  Circular economy & green transition · Academic cooperation & research ·
  Digital culture & smart communities · Youth & future skills

## 4. .Traces (Tracce) — le 15 tappe (stesse `y`, `tag`, media e pin)

- **1993 · Festival of Typical Products of the Marche · Fermo** — Fermo,
  1993: a pioneering event puts the region's food heritage on a stage of
  its own. The first job is not telling the world about the Marche — it
  is convincing the locals that what is made down the road deserves the
  spotlight. — caption: `Fermo, 1993 — from the first edition's archive.`
- **1998–2006 · Castilla y León · Spain** — Valladolid, 1998: a handshake
  between two inland regions. Then press tours, a forum on mountain
  communities and, in 2003, a cooperation pact with the institutions of
  Castilla y León signed within the festival itself: Tipicità turns out
  to be a diplomatic table, too.
- **2006–09 · Lofoten Islands · Norway** — In Svolvær, Marche chefs serve
  hundreds of portions of stockfish all'anconitana in the very port it
  has sailed from for five centuries. The deeper mark is left by the
  method: the islands founded a festival modelled on Tipicità. Products
  travel — formats take root. — caption: `Lofoten Islands — from the
  mission footage. [DRAFT]`
- **2011 · Made in Marche Festival · Fermo** — Food leads, the rest
  follows: the Made in Marche Gallery debuts at Fermo Forum — fashion
  and design showcases beside the food stalls. One regional know-how, in
  different materials. — caption: `Fermo — the festival becomes Made in
  Marche. [DRAFT]`
- **2015 · EXPO Milano · Milan** — "Feeding the Planet": Tipicità
  arrives in Milan with a concrete answer — the Long-Life Basket, built
  with the University of Camerino and producers from one of Europe's
  longest-living territories. Not a slogan: a diet that has worked for
  centuries.
- **2015–17 · Italian Cuisine World Summit · Dubai & Abu Dhabi, UAE** —
  At the summit that brings dozens of Michelin stars to the Gulf,
  Tipicità adds the missing link: not the dishes — the producers.
  Italian cuisine told by the people who grow it.
- **2018 · NY · Taste Marche Experience · New York, USA** — At Lucciola,
  an Italian outpost in New York, the Taste Marche Experience stages
  food, fashion and eco design: three codes, one know-how. The city that
  crowns brands discovers the region that makes them. — caption:
  `New York — Taste Marche Experience at Lucciola. [DRAFT]`
- **2018 · AL · 3rd Week of Italian Cuisine in the World · Tirana &
  Vlorë, Albania** — The same year, the opposite shore: Tirana and Vlorë
  for the 3rd Week of Italian Cuisine in the World. The Adriatic as a
  bridge, not a border. — caption: `Tirana — the Week of Italian
  Cuisine. [DRAFT]`
- **2019 · 4th Week of Italian Cuisine in the World · Ottawa &
  Montréal** — Ottawa and Montréal, in the land where Italian Sounding
  outsells the original: two evenings to taste the difference between
  what sounds Italian and what is. The Taste Marche Experience crosses
  the Atlantic, northbound.
- **2022 · Simply "Festival" · EXPO2020 Dubai · Dar es Salaam &
  Zanzibar** — Tipicità becomes simply "Festival", an ecosystem of
  Italy's most authentic micro-communities. At EXPO Dubai its leaf turns
  into a phygital gift box, a QR code transforming products into
  journeys. And in Dar es Salaam and Zanzibar — a first in Africa —
  Tipicità represents Italy itself. — caption: `Simply "Festival" — the
  motion poster. [DRAFT]`
- **2023 · UK · Tipicità Experience · London, United Kingdom** — The
  2023 edition launches from Hampstead: Rossodisera, London's only
  Marche kitchen, becomes the region's permanent embassy in the city.
  And the Consul reminds the guests that "typical" comes from the Greek
  týpos: imprint — a uniqueness that cannot be imitated.
- **2023 · FR · 8th Week of Italian Cuisine in the World · Paris** —
  Paris: the official presentation of Italian cuisine's candidacy for
  UNESCO intangible heritage. The local that has crossed the world asks
  for its journey to be recognised.
- **2024 · 9th Week of Italian Cuisine in the World · Tokyo & Osaka** —
  A brodetto master class for Japanese chefs, a food-science agreement
  between Ritsumeikan and Camerino universities and, in Osaka, Kumiko
  Muraji's osteria cooking marchigiano: the best routes already have
  someone travelling them in reverse. — caption: `Tokyo & Osaka — the
  9th Week. [DRAFT]`
- **2025 · 10th Week of Italian Cuisine in the World · EXPO2025 Osaka ·
  Bahía Blanca, Buenos Aires, Ushuaia** — From EXPO Osaka to the longest
  route yet: three thousand kilometres through Bahía Blanca — Fermo's
  twin city — Buenos Aires and Ushuaia, bringing Mediterranean
  biodiversity to the world's southernmost city on the eve of the UNESCO
  verdict. A year later, Argentina sets up its own stall in Fermo. —
  caption: `From Osaka to the end of the world — and back to Fermo.
  [DRAFT]`
- **2027 · 35th edition · Fermo, 12–14 March** — The route comes home:
  Fermo, 12–14 March 2027. Not to stop — to set out again.
